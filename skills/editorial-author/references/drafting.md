# Drafting: from facts to voice

## Never

```
story.json → paraphrase every field → article
```

`story.json`, `evidence.md` and `brief.md` are inputs. They say what is true and what the platform needs. They are
not a paragraph plan. Do not mirror their structure: a story with `problem / constraints / solution / results /
limitations` does not need five sections with those names.

## Instead

```
story.json + author input + platform research + author voice + style
  → editorial decisions (direction, pattern transfer) → narrative (voice plan) → article
```

Write beat by beat from the voice plan. Pull facts from the claims each beat references; check them against evidence.

## Living prose

Long-form prose must not read like documentation, release notes, an architecture inventory, a README, a PR
description or a generated executive summary. Technical accuracy is mandatory; present technical content through

```
problem → attempt → friction → discovery → design consequence
```

when the evidence supports that sequence.

- Real conflict early; context after the reader knows why it matters.
- One central episode told slowly and precisely; the rest moves.
- Code and screenshots prove a point in the story.
- Limitations where they bite.
- First person for decisions the author made; experiences only if the author supplied them.
- Author phrases: VERBATIM exactly; MUST ideas in your words or theirs.

## Do not fabricate

Tension, emotions, effort or incidents: no "I was shocked", "I spent the whole night", "users complained",
"production broke" unless the author supplied it. A calm story told well is better than invented drama.

## Platform

The platform strategy adjusts title constraints, section density, technical depth and code/media usage.
It does not change who is speaking.
